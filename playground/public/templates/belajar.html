Questions, answered

Everything about courses, certificates, and studying with a mentor.

Still unsure which course fits?

Our student advisors reply within minutes, every day from 08:00 to 21:00 WIB.

Do I need a psychology background?

No. Foundations courses start from zero. Clinical practice courses list what you should know first on each course page.

What is the difference between theory and practice hours?

Theory hours are short video lessons and readings. Practice hours are guided exercises, role plays, and case studies that a mentor reviews.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. Finish all lessons and practice tasks to receive a certificate with a verification link you can add to your CV or LinkedIn.

Can I study on my phone?

Every lesson works in the browser on phones and laptops, and you can download audio guides to listen offline.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Our courses teach knowledge and skills. If you are struggling, please reach out to a licensed psychologist or call 119 ext 8.
