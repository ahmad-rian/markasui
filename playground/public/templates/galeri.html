Questions artists ask us

How much does Sanggar take from a sale?
Starter pays a 10% fee, Artist 5%, and Studio 3%. Payment processing is included, so the price a collector pays is the price you set.
When do I receive my money?
Every Friday we send the week's earnings to your Indonesian bank account or e-wallet, as long as the balance is above Rp50.000.
Who handles shipping for physical works?
You pack the work, we book the courier and insure it. Collectors see the shipping cost at checkout before they pay.
Do I keep the rights to my art?
Always. You grant Sanggar a license to show your work on the marketplace only, and you can remove it at any time.
Can I cancel or switch plans?
Yes. Change plans from your settings at any time. Yearly plans are refunded pro rata for unused months.
