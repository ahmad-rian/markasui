Which tender portals does Tendera cover?

Every LPSE portal that runs SPSE, the LKPP e-catalogue, and the procurement pages of the larger state-owned companies. New listings show up within an hour of publication.

Will the AI write the whole proposal for me?

It writes a complete first draft from the tender document and your past proposals. Your team still reviews every section, and each paragraph links to the clause it answers.

Is my company data safe?

Files are stored in Jakarta data centres and encrypted at rest. Your documents are never used to train shared models, and you can export or delete them at any time.

Can I cancel at any time?

Yes. Monthly plans stop at the end of the billing period. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days.

Do you replace our bid consultant?

Most teams use Tendera to bring bidding in house. Some keep their consultant for very large tenders and use Tendera to prepare everything else.
